Common questions

Can I cancel any time? +
Yes — cancel from your dashboard. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period and can export everything before you go.
Who can see my videos? +
Only the people you give the link AND password to. Your site is private by default; videos never appear in search or any public feed.
What happens after my free trial? +
Your site goes into a read-only state for 30 days while you decide. Nothing is deleted — pick up exactly where you left off when you're ready.
Can I switch plans later? +
Up or down, any time. Upgrades are prorated; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
Do you have a gift option? +
Yes! Pre-pay any plan as a gift and we'll send a beautifully designed reveal card on the day of your choosing.
