Accessibility

LoveFlix is committed to making our platform accessible to everyone, regardless of ability.

Our Commitment

We continuously work to conform our service to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, and are working toward 2.2. Our goal is to ensure that all users — including those who rely on screen readers, keyboard navigation, voice control, or assistive technologies — can enjoy LoveFlix together with their partner. This statement reflects the current state of the site and is updated as we make further improvements; it is not a claim of perfect, zero-defect conformance.

ARIA Support

LoveFlix uses Accessible Rich Internet Applications (ARIA) attributes throughout the platform to improve compatibility with screen readers and other assistive technologies:

  • Skip link — A "Skip to main content" link is the first focusable element on every page, letting keyboard users bypass repeated navigation
  • aria-label — Applied to icon-only buttons and controls so their purpose is announced by screen readers
  • aria-hidden — Used on decorative elements and background visuals to reduce noise for assistive technology users
  • Landmarks — Navigation, main content, and footer regions are marked up with semantic <nav>, <main>, and <footer>/role attributes for efficient page navigation
  • aria-live / role="status" / role="log" — Dynamic content regions (chat notifications, call status, form errors) announce changes without disrupting the user
  • aria-expanded — Expandable sections (FAQ accordions, menus) communicate their open/closed state
  • role="dialog" with focus trapping — Modals and overlays keep keyboard focus contained while open and return it to the triggering control on close

Keyboard Shortcuts

The following keyboard shortcuts are available across LoveFlix:

Shortcut Action
Tab / Shift + Tab Navigate forward / backward through interactive elements
Enter or Space Activate the focused button or link
Escape Close modals, dropdowns, and overlays
Tab / Shift + Tab Move between video cards in a row; each row also has visible, focusable "scroll left/right" buttons
Arrow Keys Seek video playback (player), move focus between form choices (e.g. time-slot pickers), and pan the Browse 3D grid
F Toggle full-screen mode during video playback
M Mute / unmute audio during video playback
Space Play / pause video

Screen Reader & Assistive Technology

  • All form inputs have associated <label> elements
  • Images include descriptive alt text or are marked as decorative with alt=""
  • Text can be resized up to 200% without loss of content or functionality
  • Colour contrast ratios are targeted to meet or exceed WCAG AA requirements (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and UI components)
  • Interactive elements are focusable and display visible focus indicators

Known Limitations

We aim to be direct about where LoveFlix currently falls short of full accessibility, rather than overstate our conformance:

  • Closed captions: uploaded videos do not currently support closed captions or subtitle tracks. If you need a transcript or captions for a specific video, contact us below and we'll do our best to help.
  • 3D Browse view: the "Browse" page's interactive 3D grid is a WebGL visualization that isn't fully readable by screen readers. Use the "Switch to list view" button (reachable via Tab, top-left of the Browse page) for a standard, fully accessible list of the same content.
  • We test primarily with modern screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA) and keyboard-only navigation; if you use other assistive technology and hit a barrier, please tell us.

Report an Accessibility Issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or have suggestions for improvement, please reach out to us:

Email: accessibility@loveflix.app

Response time: We aim to respond within 2 business days

Feedback: Include Accessibility in the subject line and describe the issue, the browser/device you are using, and any assistive technology involved.
