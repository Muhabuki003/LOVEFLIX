The Story

How LoveFlix came to be — a love letter to shared experiences.

LoveFlix was born from a simple realization: couples don't have a place of their own on the internet. Most platforms are built for one user, or for the whole world — but what about the space between two people?

The idea came during a late-night video call. A couple miles apart wanted to watch a movie together, share a Spotify playlist, drop a pin of their favourite coffee shop, and send a quick selfie — all without juggling five different apps. Every existing service was either public (social media), solo (Netflix), or cluttered with noise from everyone else.

So we built it. A private Netflix-style platform — intimate, beautiful, and just for two. No ads. No algorithms feeding you content from strangers. No friends-of-friends. No noise. Just the two of you, your media, your memories, and your world.

"LoveFlix isn't another social network. It's a private sanctuary for the most important connection in your life."

Every feature — from video streaming to the shared story timeline — was designed with one question in mind: "Does this bring couples closer together?" If the answer wasn't a resounding yes, it didn't make the cut.

Simple, powerful, and made to last.

Vanilla JavaScript Cloudflare Workers Cloudflare D1 Cloudflare R2 WebRTC WebSockets

No bloated frameworks. No unnecessary dependencies. LoveFlix is built with vanilla JS and powered by Cloudflare's global edge network — fast, scalable, and resilient. Your data stays secure across Cloudflare's infrastructure with D1 for storage, R2 for media, and Workers for every API endpoint.
